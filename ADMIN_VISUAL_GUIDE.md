## 5-Minute Client Demo Script (How the Software is Easily Used)

### Minute 1: The Today Cockpit
* Open `Today Cockpit`.
* Show the personalized greeting, date, and 3 clean metric cards.
* Explain the **100-Point Monthly Productivity Score**:
  - Starts at 100/100 each month.
  - Automatically penalizes overdue tasks (-8 pts) and deadline shifts (-5 pts).
* Click a **Daily Standing Duty** to show instant streak completion.

### Minute 2: Projects & 200+ SOP Checklists
* Open `Projects & Tasks`.
* Show client projects (*Apex Financial*, *Nova Healthcare*), progress percentages, and deliverables.
* Click into **Checklists** to showcase standard operating procedures.
* Click into **Minutes of Meeting (MOM)** and click **"Export / Print MOM PDF"** to demonstrate instant branded documentation.

### Minute 3: Expenses & Invoices
* Open `Expenses & Invoices`.
* Show employee expense voucher filing and demonstrate **1-Click Admin Approval**.
* Show the client invoicing ledger with GST totals and payment due dates.

### Minute 4: The 'Showstopper' — Office TV Wallboard
* Click the **Tv** icon in the header or sidebar.
* Demonstrate the fullscreen command center displaying real-time digital clock, standing duties compliance, and campus attendance. Press `Esc` to return.

### Minute 5: Direct In-App User Activation & The Close
* Open `User Activation`.
* Demonstrate **"⚡ 1-Click Activate"** to immediately activate or suspend users.
* Pitch the pricing tier (*Starter ₹60k/yr* or *Professional ₹1.8L/yr*) and close the deal!

## Super Admin Master Operations (How to Do Everything)

1. **Managing Software Sales & Deals**:
   - Go to `Software Sales` → Click `Add ERP Sales Deal`.
   - Track inbound inquiries and update deal stages.
   - When a deal is marked "Closed Won", click **"Provision Key"** to generate a cryptographic license key: `AISH-PRO-2026-XXXX-YYYY`.

2. **Activating Users & Organizations**:
   - Go to `User Activation` → Click `Activate New User`.
   - Enter Full Name, Email, Organization, and assign their Role (`Super Admin`, `Sales Admin`, `Project Manager`, `Team Member`, `Client Portal`).
   - Use **"1-Click Activate / Deactivate"** anytime to control access immediately.

3. **Approving Expense Claims**:
   - Go to `Expenses & Invoices` → Click `Approve` on any voucher to disburse with an Admin signature.

4. **Testing Role Permissions**:
   - Use the **Role Switcher** in the top header to preview the ERP as a Project Manager or Client to verify security.
